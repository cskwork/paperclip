# Math Content Evidence

## Required inputs

- Defined topic and learner level
- Project-provided sources and curriculum trace IDs
- Claims, examples, items, or solutions to verify

## Procedure

1. Separate mathematical facts, source-dependent claims, instructional hypotheses, and open questions.
2. Recompute every numerical result independently and test sign, order, equivalent-form, zero, and boundary cases that apply.
3. Check that symbols, number-line positions, verbal explanations, and worked steps express the same relationship.
4. For each misconception, state the observable error pattern and the evidence source or label it as a design hypothesis.
5. Reject fake citations, unattributed copied items, and claims that cannot be supported by supplied or permitted sources.
6. Record corrections with the affected trace IDs and the verification method.

## Durable output

A claim-and-evidence table plus calculation log containing claim ID, type, evidence location, verification steps, result, uncertainty, and affected content IDs.

## Done checks

- Every answer and worked step reproduces correctly.
- Representations agree across forms.
- Source claims and internal hypotheses are visibly distinct.
- Unresolved mathematical uncertainty is blocking and has an owner.
